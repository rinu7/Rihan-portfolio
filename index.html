About

I make ideas move.

I’m Moiyadeen Rihan, a social media manager, Meta Ads marketer, content creator and video editor with 6 years of experience helping brands grow their audience and tell their stories through video. I shoot and edit hands-on, and use advanced AI tools to work faster and push the visuals further. I’m currently in Dubai on a 2-month visit visa and available for roles in the UAE.

I bring ideas to life through planning, shooting, editing, motion graphics, colour grading and sound design. My approach combines creative storytelling with an understanding of social media, creating content that catches attention and gives people a reason to keep watching.

From cinematic automotive showcases and real estate videos to brand campaigns, motion graphics and social media reels, I’ve delivered 1,000+ videos and generated over 10 million views. Every project starts with understanding the story and finding the right way to tell it.
